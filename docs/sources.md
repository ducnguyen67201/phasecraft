# Sources and provenance

[Back to Phasecraft](../README.md)

Phasecraft was extracted from a human-led engineering collaboration with separate coordinator and phase-owner conversations. The public examples are fictional and omit private chat identifiers, user paths, credentials, runtime details, and raw transcripts.

The core lessons are operational: bounded ownership, isolated writers, real dependency contracts, owner-led verification, scoped human approvals, durable evidence, and retirement after verified merge. They are a documented practice, not evidence that the method is novel or faster than alternatives.

[ECC, affaan-m/ecc](https://github.com/affaan-m/ecc) was the user's reference for distributing an engineering toolbox and usage guidance. Its public repository was reviewed on 2026-10-29. ECC describes a broader collection of agents, skills, rules, memory, and supporting runtime tools. Phasecraft has a narrower scope: the coordination procedure and reusable records. No ECC source or installer is included, and no endorsement or affiliation is implied.

A locally installed Skill Creator guide informed the packaging conventions. A local Phase Coordinator skill informed the procedure, generalized here so project-specific preferences remain policy choices. Archify generated the diagram from its editable JSON; its regenerable viewer and delivery receipts stay local. Exported renderer styling retains its [third-party notice](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Do not add real private project records as examples. Submit sanitized, independently understandable cases, with the permission needed to share any original material.
