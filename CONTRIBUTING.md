# Contributing

Start with a concrete engineering situation and the decision the procedure handled poorly. Describe the expected result, actual result, relevant policy, and evidence. Remove credentials, personal data, private chat IDs, and unrelated project details.

Keep the portable skill concise. Put longer explanations in guides, and keep host-specific behavior separate from the general procedure. Do not add permissions through an example or template. Project choices about testing, UI gates, publication, and deployment must remain explicit choices.

For changes, check local links, parse JSON, validate the sample registry against its schema, and review whether examples still match the instructions. For skill changes, try a realistic task in an isolated workspace and record what happened. Do not claim host compatibility or measured improvements without evidence.

Architecture proposals should distinguish practiced behavior from proposed software. Maintain diagram sources and readable artifacts together. Keep generated viewers, delivery sidecars, and temporary review captures out of commits.

Contributions are submitted under the repository's MIT license. Do not copy third-party material without preserving its license and attribution.
